Little Yellow Journals

Voices from Sacred Places

Tucked beneath every Nature Sacred Bench is a Little Yellow Journal—a place for visitors to pause, reflect, and leave something behind. Since 1996, these anonymous entries have captured everything from everyday observations to deeply personal moments, offering a remarkable window into what happens when people find a place to pause in nature.

Here, you'll find ways to use and share your journals, return completed journals to the Nature Sacred archive, and request more when you need them.

How Nature Sacred uses journal entries

Little Yellow Journal entries give us a firsthand window into what people experience in Sacred Places. We use these reflections to tell the story of the Network, demonstrate the impact of Sacred Places, inspire others, and help funders and supporters understand why these places—and the Firesouls™ who care for them—matter.

How you can use your entries

Share the Stories. Show the Impact.

Journal entries are powerful, firsthand testimonials of what your Sacred Place means to visitors. You can use them to:

Support fundraising — strengthen grants, donor appeals, and reports.

Tell your story — create meaningful social media, website, and newsletter content.

Show your impact — share visitors' own words with funders, partners, and supporters.

Amplify together — share entries with Nature Sacred so we can collaborate on storytelling and help your Sacred Place reach a wider audience.

Share & restock your journals

Collection tips & ideas

Every Sacred Place is different, and sometimes keeping journals at the bench takes a little creativity. Visitors may take journals with them—and that's okay. We'll always send you more.

Firesouls across the Network have found different approaches that work for their spaces:

Bring journals in at night and put them back out each morning.
Put journals out during programs and events when visitors are most likely to use them.
Tether your journal to the bench. Nature Sacred can provide tethered journals and a simple installation video.
Encourage your community to share their stories

Sometimes all it takes is a little invitation:

Mention the journal at the beginning or end of programs and events.

Build journaling into programming with a prompt or community reflection activity.

Share journal entries on social media to introduce the journal and encourage others to add their voices.

Create your own journal moments that reflect your community and Sacred Place.

Need ideas? Nature Sacred is here to brainstorm with you. We can help you think through ways to keep journals accessible, encourage participation, or incorporate them into your programming.
