What happens here

Neighbors use the tree canopy on hot days, hold intimate conversations, and organize small group cook-outs. Donald has begun a well-loved tradition of seasonal and community festivals in the space.

01

The Need

The expansive demolition of deteriorating vacant homes left scars along the block, and chronic illegal dumping followed.

02

The Vision

Residents of the 1800 Block of Saratoga, led by Firesoul Donald Quarles, set out to stop the dumping, restore the block, and establish a positive space to relax and enjoy the outdoors. Members of the community brought their own hopes for the park, drawing a synergy between the block’s cherished pastimes and the serenity prayer.

03

The Transformation

The community began the work themselves — neighborhood cleanups, pruning the bushes, installing natural fencing, and creating log seating. The result accommodates adults and children alike.
